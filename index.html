Questions

Quick answers before you sail.

What is Decklumos?

Decklumos puts every deck of your ship in your pocket and on your G2 glasses, with venues, port guides and your trip plans.

Do I need Even Realities G2 glasses to use Decklumos?

No! Decklumos works directly on your phone. If you own Even Realities G2 glasses, you can also view your ship's deck map in your field of vision.

Does Decklumos work offline?

Yes! Deck maps, port guides and your trips are built into the app, so they work with no Wi-Fi or signal. Cruise News needs a connection to load the latest stories.

Which cruise ships does Decklumos support?

Decklumos supports 167 ships across 10 cruise lines, including Royal Caribbean, Carnival, Celebrity Cruises, Disney Cruise Line, MSC Cruises, Norwegian Cruise Line, and more. Check the app to see if your ship is supported.

Is Decklumos free to download?

Yes! Decklumos is free.
