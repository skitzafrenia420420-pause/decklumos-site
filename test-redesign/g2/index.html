Questions

G2 questions

Do I need G2 glasses to use Decklumos?

No. Decklumos works right on your phone. The glasses are an optional extra.

Which glasses does it work with?

Even Realities G2 glasses.

Does it work offline on the glasses?

Yes. Deck maps and your trips are built in, so they work with no Wi-Fi or signal. Cruise News needs a connection.

Is it free?

Yes. Decklumos is free.

Do I need a Decklumos account?

No. Everything works without signing in, on your phone and on G2. Creating an account is optional.

Something looks wrong on my glasses?

Email the Owners at support@decklumos.com. We read every message.
