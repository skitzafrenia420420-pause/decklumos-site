Decklumos privacy policy

Last updated: October 23, 2026

Decklumos shows built-in deck maps for 167 cruise ships across 10 cruise lines: Carnival, Celebrity, Disney, MSC, Norwegian, Princess, Royal Caribbean, Viking Ocean Cruises, Viking River Cruises and Virgin Voyages. It works on your phone and on Even Realities G2 glasses. Decklumos is not affiliated with any cruise line.

The short version

The app collects no personal data. The only thing it loads from the internet is Cruise News, from decklumos.com. There is no account, no sign-in, no tracking, no ads and no location.

What stays on your phone

  • Your trip plans (My Trips)
  • Your all-aboard time
  • The cruise line and ship you picked
  • The port shown on your glasses

These are saved only on your phone, in the Even Realities app's local storage on your phone. They are never uploaded to us or anyone else. Removing the app deletes them.

Snap a plan

When you snap a photo of a cruise plan, Decklumos reads the text on your phone. The photo never leaves your device and is never uploaded to us or anyone else.

This website

decklumos.com is hosted by GitHub Pages, which may keep standard server logs (like IP address, browser type and pages visited) to run and protect the site. We don't add analytics or tracking cookies.

The Cruise News links go to outside publishers. Their sites have their own privacy policies.

Children

Decklumos does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children under 13.
