What is Decklumos?

Decklumos shows built-in deck maps for 167 cruise ships across 10 cruise lines, plus port guides, My Trips plans and Cruise News. It works on your phone and on Even Realities G2 glasses, and deck maps and port guides work fully offline. Decklumos is not affiliated with any cruise line.
